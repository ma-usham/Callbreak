# Architecture decision records

GDD v1.1 §16 lists ADRs 001-009. The records here are the decisions taken while building the game
(30 Sep 2026) where the implementation needed a choice the GDD didn't make, or departs from it.
Each says what was decided, why, and what it would take to change it. The owner can overrule any
of them; the GDD is updated once a decision is confirmed.

| ADR | Decision | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [010](0010-screens-rebuilt-from-their-own-mockups.md) | Screens are laid out on their own mockups, but share one background and one 9-slice UI kit | Accepted (owner) |
| [011](0011-task-based-ports.md) | Application ports use `Task`, not Unity `Awaitable` | Proposed |
| [012](0012-csv-string-tables.md) | Localisation from CSV string tables behind `ILocalization`, not the Unity Localization package, for v1 | Proposed |
| [013](0013-no-records-in-first-party-code.md) | No `record` types or `init` accessors in first-party code | Proposed |
| [014](0014-card-faces-cut-from-reference-sheets.md) | Card faces are cut from the reference sheets at their real proportions | Proposed |
| [015](0015-local-services-before-ugs.md) | Local adapters for identity, saves and analytics until a Unity Cloud project is linked | Proposed |
| [016](0016-online-protocol-behind-byte-links.md) | The online protocol is transport-free C#; PurrNet's relay transport only carries its bytes (addendum: the online review, protocol 2, what is still open) | Proposed |
| [017](0017-system-fonts-behind-the-game-fonts.md) | The device's fonts stand behind the game's Latin-only fonts; suit symbols are sprites | Proposed |
| [018](0018-first-launch-and-rejoin-flows.md) | What the menu starts by itself and in which order: invite link, rejoin, resume, first-run guide | Proposed |
| [019](0019-menu-states-the-mockups-do-not-show.md) | Menu states no mockup draws: the Resume banner, the fan's sway, failures with Retry, dialogs that can be closed, UI sound cues | Proposed |
| [020](0020-one-deck-per-table.md) | One deck per table, every face and back: offline the player's deck, a private room the host's, Quick Match one drawn from the catalog (replaces GDD §13's deck per seat) | Accepted (owner) |
| [021](0021-one-hand-order-and-the-emote-button-on-the-right.md) | The hand is always ♠ ♥ ♣ ♦ with no Sort button, the emote button takes its place on the right, and the hand's arc bends half as much (replaces GDD §2's two orders) | Accepted (owner) |
| [022](0022-name-at-the-start-thrown-cards-and-a-shuffled-deal.md) | The name is asked when the game starts; cards are thrown from the hand, not tapped; the deck is shuffled in the middle of the table and dealt from there; "Resume your offline match?" | Accepted (owner) |
| [023](0023-ui-motion-with-litmotion.md) | One way of moving for the whole UI, with LitMotion: screens slide and grow, panels pop in and out, the menu's buttons rise, the table's parts appear softly; only fades with Reduce motion (replaces GDD §14's screen change) | Accepted (owner) |
| [024](0024-own-phrase-cooldown-and-straight-trick.md) | Your own quick phrase shows by the emote button, which shows the 3 s wait as a sweep; the trick cards lie straight and the winner does not grow; the bots wait for the shuffle and the deal (replaces GDD §11's ±6° and §14's lift) | Accepted (owner) |
| [025](0025-online-own-deck-and-no-bot-tags.md) | Quick Match plays in the player's own deck (replaces ADR 020's deck drawn from the catalog); online, a bot that fills a seat is shown as a player: "Ready" in the search, no BOT chip at the table (replaces GDD §9's BOT tag) | Accepted (owner) |
| [026](0026-opponent-fans-and-bots-that-chat.md) | Opponent fans as mockup 07 now draws them (vertical arcs beside the side seats, a hanging arc below the top seat); online, the bots that fill seats say a quick phrase now and then, at moments that fit | Accepted (owner) |
| [027](0027-seats-speech-boxes-and-fans-that-close-up.md) | The side seats lower, the top seat's name above its avatar, the player's below; the hand's cards 9% bigger; every quick phrase, the player's own too, in an ivory speech box over the speaker (replaces ADR 024's bubble by the button); side fans close up as cards are played, and dealt cards land where they stay | Accepted (owner) |
| [028](0028-online-rooms-over-epic-online-services.md) | Online rooms over Epic Online Services, peer to peer: device sign-in, lobbies as the room-code directory, P2P with Epic's free relays (replaces ADR 016's PurrNet relay, which may not ship; PurrNet stays as a development fallback) | Accepted (owner) |
| [029](0029-quick-match-finds-real-players.md) | Quick Match finds real players over EOS lobbies: search first, join or host; a 20 s countdown (14 s people only, then bots fill until full); a locked 3-2-1 shared by every screen; each player sees themselves first (replaces ADR 016's local bot-only Quick Match and GDD §11 screen 05's elapsed clock) | Accepted (owner) |
| [030](0030-host-loss-waiting-room-and-hand-over.md) | When the host is lost: in the Quick Match waiting room everyone matchmakes again (1 s heartbeat, 3 s silence); in a match the next host in line takes over and the round goes on from the same trick (the host shares bot cards with that player, an owner-approved exception); EOS shuts down safely before the plugin releases it | Accepted (owner) |
| [031](0031-my-deck-everywhere-and-a-quieter-table.md) | Every player sees every card in their own chosen deck (supersedes ADR 020/025 table decks); a quieter table: no Trump label, a won-bid seat pill, no call bubble, no chip or toast for players who left, the loading emblem for Reconnecting, any touch ends Away; Back to Game online; no gear on the Settings page. Addendum: no chip for a seat not back in time, gold whoever plays the seat, pills that fit their text, and "Reconnecting…" only where a player can come back (private-room hosts keep a rejoin ticket; Quick Match shows nothing) | Accepted (owner) |
| [032](0032-backup-host-no-unfair-loss-and-fields-above-the-keyboard.md) | A backup host: the k-th in line takes over after (k−1)×10 s and deals the round again (protocol 7; card-free packages for later successors); no loss when the match dies while this device was online; text fields show their cursor on phones and rise above the keyboard (name, Profile, Create Room, Join Room) | Accepted (owner) |
| [033](0033-shop-coins-and-swappable-table-scenery.md) | The Shop (Cards, Backgrounds, Tables: drawer and carousels) replaces Card Collection; gold coins (start 500, three rewarded ads per 5 h, a stand-in until the owner picks an ad network); the table's backdrop is three layers (scenery, table, foreground) showing each player's own equipped background and table | Accepted (owner) |
| [034](0034-stake-match-and-admob-rewarded-ads.md) | Play Online offers Quick (free) or a 50/100-coin Stake Match: prizes by place, bots win nothing, tied places each get the prize, leaving forfeits, one EOS bucket per stake; real AdMob rewarded ads on phones (test units in development builds, Google's consent first). Addendum: the "+N coins" results line, chips that remember the last stake, Privacy options in Settings | Accepted (owner) |
| [035](0035-no-ads-and-ads-between-matches.md) | No Ads, a $0.99 Play purchase (Unity IAP, product `no_ads`, a button on the main menu), removes new full-screen AdMob ads shown as the player leaves a finished match's results (none on a private room's Play Again, after the first match, or within 3 minutes of the last); the Earn Coins videos stay; R8 keep rules for the Billing classes | Accepted (owner) |
| [036](0036-forced-updates-from-google-play.md) | Any newer version on Google Play is installed through Play's immediate in-app update, asked at launch and on the main menu (never mid-match, in a room, or while a rejoin may come); backing out shows a non-dismissable "Update required" | Accepted (owner) |
