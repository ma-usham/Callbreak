## Classic Crimson

Output: [classic-crimson-four-aces.png](classic-crimson-four-aces.png)

```text
Use case: product-mockup / game card-face design.
Create a high-quality CARD FACE SAMPLE SHEET for the Nepali Callbreak game. Input references show the existing game's three deck BACK designs and its gameplay card faces. Use these as design context, not as layouts to reproduce. This request is for four actual FRONT faces of one deck theme.
Composition: a clean 2048x1152 landscape presentation board, uniform warm-neutral parchment background with no landscape scenery. Exactly FOUR separate portrait playing-card FRONT faces in ONE horizontal row, all identical size and poker proportions approximately 5:7, straight upright, perfectly front-on, no perspective, no rotations, no overlap. Large cards with crisp sharp readable typography, equal spacing, full rounded corners visible, balanced generous board margins and very subtle grounded shadows. The theme title is above the row. Beneath each card a small plain readable caption respectively "Ace of Spades", "Ace of Hearts", "Ace of Diamonds", "Ace of Clubs".
Card order left to right MUST be A♠, A♥, A♦, A♣. Each card has exactly one large recognizable standard suit symbol in the center: spade with stem, heart with two lobes and bottom point, straight-sided diamond, three-leaf club with stem. Oversized but tasteful crisp serif "A" stacked over a small matching suit symbol at TOP LEFT. The same A and suit index are rotated together 180 degrees at BOTTOM RIGHT, as on a standard reversible playing card. The bottom-right index must be genuinely upside-down. Leave safe inset margins and clear space around both indices so they read in a fanned hand. No other letters or ranks and no additional central pips. All four faces share the exact same border geometry, paper texture, layout, index scale and corner radius. Preserve clear suit-color distinctions. Artwork must look intentionally designed for a full 52-card game deck, with restrained detail and excellent small-screen readability.
Art direction: the supplied game has warm ivory paper, deep crimson, antique gold, and restrained Nepali woven geometric diamond patterns. Use only geometric ornament, NO FLOWERS, floral damask, leaves, vines, rosettes, or visible botanical patterns. Keep decoration at the borders and faint in the material; the central field stays clean. No temple silhouettes or pictures inside suit icons, no complex heraldic reinterpretations of pips. No giant mandalas. No shiny neon glow, no rainbow foil, no chunky 3D objects, no card-back spade medallions mistaken for face layouts. Do not reproduce shop UI, controls, prices, avatars, logos, or the CALLBREAK heading. Output the complete board only.

THEME: CLASSIC CRIMSON. Header text exactly "Classic Crimson", in a dignified dark burgundy serif. Design luxurious but playable faces related to the LEFT crimson card back in reference. Each face has an elegant rich crimson outer frame with a very fine antique-gold keyline. A few small tonal Nepali woven diamond accents integrated into the crimson frame at top/bottom corners only. The main face and both index areas are smooth warm ivory paper, occupying at least 85 percent of the card area, with just perceptible paper grain. Use deep charcoal-black ranks and pips for spades/clubs and rich crimson-red ranks and pips for hearts/diamonds. A single clean bold central pip on each face, with a hint of print texture rather than gloss. Classic, welcoming, refined, readily usable in the existing cream/red game. No visually obvious ornament or flower texture in the ivory center. Thin refined borders, not fat red bands. Four consistent ACES, no other rank.
```
