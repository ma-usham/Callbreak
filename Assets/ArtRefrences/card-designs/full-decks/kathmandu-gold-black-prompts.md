# Kathmandu Gold — restored black faces

Mode: built-in image editor (`image_gen`), style-transfer edits.

The latest user request restores the original black/charcoal card faces, bright gold spades/clubs, and crimson hearts/diamonds with fine yellow-gold outlines around both ranks and suit symbols. These four sheets replace the prior ivory-faced Kathmandu Gold deck in the gallery. The earlier light sheets are preserved under `archive/kathmandu-gold-ivory/`.

Keep both indices and rotate bottom right indices by 180 degrees. Do not add or remove any pip. No change to layouts, no overlap, no cropped cards, no new words, no jokers. Restore the black-faced style, not just a dark border.
